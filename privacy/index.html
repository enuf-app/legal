This summary is for convenience. The full Privacy Policy below applies.

This Privacy Policy explains how Enuf, an iOS app provided by Roman Petliakov, an individual developer (“Enuf,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the app. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

1. Who Is Responsible

Roman Petliakov is the data controller for Enuf. You can contact us at any time at hey.enuf@gmail.com.

2. Information That Stays on Your iPhone

Enuf is built to work on your device. The following information is created and stored only on your iPhone, and is not sent to us:

  • Onboarding answers: your first name (optional), your age, what you have tried before, and how many hours a day you spend in the apps you choose.
  • Your commitment signature, if you sign it during onboarding. It is kept only in the app’s memory to show on your plan and is not saved.
  • Your app choices and usage in Enuf: which apps you hide, your sessions, timers, stats, and settings.

Deleting Enuf removes this information from your device. It may also be included in your device backups, which are controlled by you and Apple.

3. Screen Time and Your Apps

Enuf uses Apple’s Screen Time technology (Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity) to hide the apps you choose and to run your sessions. This works entirely on your device.

  • Apps you choose with Apple’s picker are given to Enuf as private tokens. We cannot see their names or your activity in them.
  • To show popular apps you already have installed, Enuf checks on your device whether a small fixed list of apps (such as TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube) is installed. This check is not sent anywhere.
  • Enuf does not read your Screen Time reports, messages, contacts, photos, location, or the content of any app.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Enuf schedules reminders on your device, for example before a session ends or before a free trial ends. These are local notifications. No notification content is sent to us.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

All payments are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never receive your name, payment card, or billing details. Apple and our paywall provider tell the app whether you have an active subscription, a trial, or an introductory offer, so that the app can unlock its features. Apple’s handling of your information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Superwall (Our Paywall Provider)

We use Superwall, a service by Superwall, Inc., to show our subscription screens and to understand how they perform. When the app runs, Superwall may receive:

  • Device and app information: a random app-specific identifier, device model, iOS version, app version, language, region, currency, time zone, and install date.
  • Network information: your IP address and an approximate location derived from it (for example, country or city).
  • Paywall and purchase activity: paywall views, which plan you choose, purchases, trials, and subscription status.
  • A few values from the app to personalize the paywall: your rounded daily hours (for example, “5h”), a goal date, an estimate of time you could get back (for example, “9.7 years”), the number of apps you chose, whether you can still get an introductory offer, and the price shown per week. Your name and your age are not sent.

Superwall processes this information to show the right paywall, process purchase status, and provide us with aggregated statistics. Superwall does not use it to track you for advertising. Learn more in Superwall’s Privacy Policy.

7. App Icons

To display app icons, Enuf may load public icon images for the apps on its fixed list from Apple’s App Store services. Like any internet request, this shares your IP address and region with Apple. It does not include which apps you have installed or hidden.

8. Crash Reports from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and basic usage statistics about Enuf with us. Apple provides this information without identifying you. You can turn it off at any time in the same place.

9. Contacting Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them only to reply and to help you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

10. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not have accounts or ask you to sign in.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not use Apple’s advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for advertising.
  • We do not currently use analytics tools in the app. If we add them, we will update this Privacy Policy first.

11. Why We Use Information (Legal Bases)

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide Enuf and your subscription.
  • Legitimate interests: to show and improve our subscription screens, keep the app working, and prevent misuse, in ways that respect your privacy.
  • Your consent: for permissions you choose to grant, such as Screen Time and notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in iOS Settings.

12. How Long Information Is Kept

Information on your device is kept until you delete it or delete Enuf. Information received by Superwall is kept according to Superwall’s Privacy Policy and our settings with them, and only as long as needed for the purposes above. Emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

13. International Transfers

Superwall and Apple may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because Enuf has no accounts, most of your information exists only on your device, and you can delete it by deleting the app. For information held by Superwall, contact us and we will help with your request. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

15. Children

Enuf is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The answers you give during onboarding stay on your device. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Security

We keep the information we handle to a minimum. Information stored on your iPhone is protected by iOS security features, such as your passcode and data encryption. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when we add new features or services. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date. If changes are significant, we will let you know in the app or on this page.

18. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy can be sent to hey.enuf@gmail.com.
